ALL INCLUSIVE — FIRST PLAYABLE

A small family-resort management game inspired by all-inclusive holidays.

ART
Original pixel artwork and character animation for this project.
Source generator: assets/art/generate_art.mjs
Original pixel UI icons: scripts/resort_ui.gd
Original modular buildings and furnishings: scripts/building_renderer.gd
Original construction menu icons: scripts/design_ui.gd

MUSIC AND SOUND
Original synthesized music and interaction effects for this project.
Source generator: assets/audio/generate_audio.py
Original synthesized seaside ambience: tools/generate_ambience.py
No third-party music recordings or sampled commercial tracks are included.

ENGINE
Built with Godot Engine 4.7.2.
https://godotengine.org/
Engine and bundled component notices are in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

The game uses system fonts where available, with Godot's bundled fallback.
