# All Inclusive — first playable acceptance

**Historical first-playable targets.** These checks describe the original fixed resort and $420 upgrade. Revision 3 construction, dynamic layouts and physical pool furnishing use the current model/integration test suites and `revision-3-validation.md`; do not reintroduce fixed geometry or the old one-click upgrade to satisfy historical fixtures.

Use the Windows export for a complete smoke test. Record the build tested, result and evidence for each check. The values below are targets for the first playable.

| Check | Test and expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Launch and onboarding | Open the exported game from a clean folder. The welcome screen identifies the facilities, objectives and controls. Start the run and see Day 1, $140, Standard price, four free bedrooms, and the float at the buffet. At 1280 × 720, labels and controls fit on screen. |
| Full guest loop | At 1×, follow at least one family from arrival to reception, bedroom, buffet, pool, bedroom and checkout. Confirm that a child companion walks with the adult, the family shares one visible queue position, charges once at check-in, occupies one room and releases it at checkout, and leaves one review. On a full Standard day, see four separate families and four reviews. |
| Package changes | Set Budget before 00:20 and confirm four check-ins bring in $360. Set $170 Premium after 00:20 and check that the current bookings retain their locked price. On the next day, see three Premium bookings bring in $510. Verify the package displays its effective day, booking count and value effect. |
| Staffing | On fresh Standard runs, compare the float at buffet with the float at pool before arrivals. In each case, see two active service slots at the selected station. With the float at the buffet, the pool wait peaks at 34 seconds. With the float at the pool, the pool has no wait and the buffet wait peaks at 30 seconds. Reassign during active service and confirm the families in service finish while new admissions use the revised capacity. |
| Terrace and money | With Standard and no purchases, verify $620 after all Day 1 check-ins and $500 after payroll. Day 1 shows the $420 upgrade with an “Available Day 2” lock. Buy it at Day 2 opening for a balance of $80. Verify the visible terrace, one additional pool slot and a disabled purchase button. On a Budget run, verify a $40 shortfall at Day 2 opening and purchase availability after the first check-in. |
| Feedback | With Standard, the float at the buffet and the base pool, the four Day 1 review scores are 92, 89, 84 and 78. The three penalized reviews identify the pool line. Install the upgrade before Day 2 arrivals, retain the float at the buffet and see 100 for each Standard checkout. The displayed average and completed count update on each checkout. |
| Clock and playtime | Verify Pause holds game time, guest positions, payroll and service progress. Check that 2× and 4× run at the selected rates and that Escape pauses and restores the previous clock state on resume. From Start to recap takes roughly 12 minutes at 1× without pauses. Verify higher speeds preserve the same booking, service and payroll events. At each day boundary, the completed day has zero occupied rooms and zero guests queued or in service, including after mid-day staff reassignments. |
| Persistence | During an active Standard day, save with guests already queued or in service. Exit the game, reopen and load. Check the same day/time, cash, active and pending price, staffing, rooms, upgrade, guest positions, reviews and chosen speed. Load opens paused. Resume to confirm that each booking, review, purchase and payroll posts at most once. Check that a fresh profile disables Load, and that an unreadable save leaves a running game intact with a clear error. |
| Final recap and recovery | Finish three Standard days with the float at the buffet and the terrace installed at Day 2 opening. The recap shows 12 completed stays, an average of 95.25 before display rounding, $800 and all targets met. Run again without buying the terrace and verify that the recap identifies the missed goal and offers a fresh run. |
| Presentation | Check the fixed three-quarter pixel-art view, distinct facility silhouettes, readable guest and queue cues, walk and swim activity, animated water, visible keyboard focus and a non-color cue for locked or selected controls. Hear looping music and distinct check-in, purchase and day-close cues, and verify mute. |
| Windows package | Launch the delivered executable with its companion files if any, outside the source tree and without opening the Godot editor. Save, close, relaunch and load from that package. |
