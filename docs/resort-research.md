# All-inclusive amenity research

Reviewed 14 November 2026 for the playable resort expansion. These are examples from official resort operators, not a claim that every hotel includes every activity. Availability and additional charges vary by property.

## Evidence

| Offering | Official examples | Game implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food and drinks throughout the resort | Beaches describes meals, snacks, and several bar settings, including oceanside and swim-up bars. | Give guests a visible included-drink stop; construction is paid by the hotel, without charging visitors per drink. |
| Beach and pool activities | Dreams Playa Mujeres lists beach/pool volleyball, table games, dance classes and non-motorized water sports. | Start with a volleyball court and beach relaxation, using placed amenities and reachable routes. |
| Entertainment | Dreams lists musical performances, theme nights and stage shows; Beaches describes live shows, bands and organized beach/pool activities. | Add an outdoor entertainment venue and visible guest participation. |
| Family activities | Dreams lists supervised clubs, crafts and sandcastle contests. Beaches also advertises kids' and teen programs. | Keep this prototype suitable for families; drinks can be juices/mocktails and beach activity can include children. |
| Premium extras | Beaches distinguishes optional spa treatments, tours and cabanas from included offerings. | Defer a separate paid-excursion/spa economy rather than implying every amenity costs guests extra. |

Sources: [Beaches inclusions](https://www.beaches.com/all-inclusive/what-is-included/), [Dreams Playa Mujeres activities](https://www.hyattinclusivecollection.com/en/resorts-hotels/dreams/mexico/playa-mujeres-golf-spa-resort/activities/), [Beaches entertainment](https://www.beaches.com/all-inclusive/entertainment/).

## Design interpretation for this iteration

The useful pattern is a choice of relaxation, sport, refreshments and entertainment within walking distance. Adding many decorative objects alone would miss the holiday experience. Prioritize a small set of venues with distinct guest behavior, legible animations and an observable benefit.

- **Usable beach:** families leave the hotel grounds, walk to reachable beach furniture, relax and return. Retain the fixed coastline. A usable beach does not require ocean swimming or shoreline editing.
- **Beach volleyball:** an editable court, a net and moving ball, with guest participation tied to an actual reserved activity.
- **Beach bar / resort bar:** placeable refreshment venues, visible drinks and visitors, with included service appropriate to a family hotel.
- **Outdoor entertainment:** a compact stage, performers and guests watching. Time and lighting should make the event distinguishable from ordinary daytime service.
- **Management:** show what is available, who is using it and why an upgrade helps. Regular construction costs and free Developer mode remain consistent with the existing editor.

Use original project artwork and sounds. Research informs behavior and amenity selection; no hotel logos, photographs, branded characters or proprietary media are copied.

## Deliberately deferred

Water sports and ocean swimming need additional safety, shoreline and animation rules. Childcare needs family splitting and supervision states. Spas, excursions, specialty restaurants and large waterparks need more space and a stronger long-term economy. Those are expansion candidates after the beach leisure loop is tested, rather than unfinished controls in this build.
